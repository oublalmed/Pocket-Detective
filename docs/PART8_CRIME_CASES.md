## 2. CASE #001 — THE SILENT WITNESS

### 2.1 Synopsis

Renowned photographer **Julien Moreau** (33) is found dead in his studio at 12 Rue de Rivoli, Paris. The player investigates his phone to discover who killed him and why.

### 2.2 Characters

| Character | Role | Age | Occupation | Relationship |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Julien Moreau | Victim | 33 | Photographer | — |
| Sophie Martin | Suspect | 28 | Gallery Owner | Ex-girlfriend |
| Marc Duval | **Culprit** | 35 | Business Partner | Childhood friend |

### 2.3 Timeline of Events

```
20:15  Marc calls Julien about accounts
20:30  Marc arrives at studio (GPS confirmed)
21:00  Marc claims he left (LIE — still there)
21:15  Sophie arrives at Café Luna
21:30  Sophie at Café Luna (GPS confirmed)
21:33  Julien texts Sophie: "Marc is leaving at 21:00"
21:45  Security photo: Marc's car STILL at studio
22:00  Marc's GPS: still at studio (murder window)
22:15  Sophie arrives at studio (finds nothing unusual)
22:30  Marc arrives home (GPS)
```

### 2.4 Evidence Map

| Evidence Type | Item | Key Clue |
|---|---|---|
| Messages | Julien ↔ Sophie thread | Marc's supposed 21:00 departure |
| Photos | Studio exterior (security cam) | Marc's car at 21:45 |
| Photos | Julien's desk | Life insurance document |
| Calls | Marc → Julien (20:15) | Marc planned visit at 20:30 |
| GPS | Sophie's phone | Alibi: Café Luna at 21:30 |
| GPS | Marc's phone | Still at studio at 22:00 |
| Documents | Life insurance policy | Marc is €500K beneficiary |

### 2.5 Solution Logic

The player must connect these evidence chains:

1. **Timeline Contradiction:** Marc claimed to leave at 21:00, but his car was photographed at 21:45 AND GPS shows him at the studio until 22:00.
2. **Financial Motive:** Marc is the sole beneficiary of Julien's €500,000 life insurance policy.
3. **Confession Under Pressure:** When confronted with both timeline and motive evidence, Marc admits to an argument about money.

**Required connections for accusation:**
- Marc's Schedule → Marc's Car (contradiction)
- Marc's Car → Marc at Studio Late (confirmation)
- Marc at Studio Late → Insurance Motive (motive + opportunity)

### 2.6 Red Herrings

- Sophie's time contradiction (said 22:00, GPS shows café at 21:30) — resolves as alibi confirmation
- Sophie's motive (Julien was switching galleries) — explored but ultimately exonerating

### 2.7 Teaching Moments (Tutorial Case)

This case introduces the player to:
- Reading message threads and identifying clue-worthy text
- Inspecting photos with hotspots
- Listening to call transcripts
- Reading GPS logs and comparing timestamps
- Making connections on the evidence board
- Interrogating suspects with and without evidence
- Making an accusation with supporting evidence

## 3. CASE #002 — DIGITAL GHOSTS

### 3.1 Synopsis

Tech CEO **Elena Vasquez** (41), founder of AI startup NovaTech, is found dead in her home office. The eerie twist: her phone continued sending automated messages for three days after her death. Someone with technical knowledge scheduled messages to create a false timeline.

### 3.2 Characters

| Character | Role | Age | Occupation | Relationship |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Elena Vasquez | Victim | 41 | Tech CEO (NovaTech) | — |
| Daniel Vasquez | Suspect | 44 | Financial Advisor | Husband |
| Priya Sharma | **Culprit** | 36 | CTO / Co-Founder | Business partner |
| Ryan Cole | Suspect | 29 | Personal Assistant | Employee |

### 3.3 Core Mystery

Elena was about to force Priya out of NovaTech at an upcoming board meeting. Priya, the CTO, used her technical skills to:
1. Kill Elena in her home office
2. Schedule delayed messages from Elena's phone to make it look like Elena was alive hours later
3. Create a false alibi by making it appear she was at home while the "living" Elena was still texting

### 3.4 Timeline of Events

```
18:00  Elena arrives home from office
18:30  Priya arrives at Elena's house (GPS)
19:00  Daniel leaves for business dinner (confirmed)
19:15  Elena's death (estimated by coroner)
19:30  Priya leaves Elena's house (GPS)
19:45  Priya arrives at her own apartment (GPS)
20:00  Scheduled message #1 sent from Elena's phone (to Daniel)
20:30  Ryan calls Elena — no answer (voicemail)
21:00  Scheduled message #2 sent from Elena's phone (to Ryan)
21:30  Daniel returns home, finds Elena
```

### 3.5 Evidence Map

| Evidence Type | Item | Key Clue |
|---|---|---|
| Messages | Elena ↔ Daniel thread | Messages sent AFTER death |
| Messages | Elena ↔ Priya thread | Board meeting confrontation |
| Messages | Elena ↔ Ryan thread | Scheduling anomalies |
| Photos | Home office security cam | Priya's car at 18:30 |
| Photos | Parking garage cam | Priya leaving at 19:30 |
| Calls | Ryan → Elena (20:30) | No answer despite "active" texting |
| Calls | Daniel ↔ Elena | Daniel's alibi confirmed |
| GPS | Priya's phone | At Elena's house 18:30-19:30 |
| GPS | Daniel's phone | At restaurant during murder window |
| Documents | Board meeting minutes draft | Priya being ousted |
| Documents | NovaTech shareholder agreement | Priya loses everything if fired |

### 3.6 Solution Logic

1. **Scheduled Messages:** Elena's phone sent messages after the estimated time of death — only someone technical could set this up.
2. **Priya's Presence:** GPS and security photos place Priya at Elena's house during the murder window.
3. **Motive:** Board documents prove Priya was about to lose her position, equity, and life's work.
4. **Technical Capability:** Only Priya (as CTO) had the skills to schedule automated messages convincingly.

**Required connections for accusation:**
- Scheduled messages anomaly → Priya's technical background
- Priya's GPS at scene → Time of death window
- Board meeting ouster → Shareholder agreement (financial ruin motive)

### 3.7 Red Herrings

- Daniel's life insurance policy — obvious suspect misdirection
- Ryan being fired — gives motive but no opportunity or technical skill
- Elena's "active" texting after death — designed to confuse timeline

### 3.8 New Mechanics Introduced

- **Scheduled messages** — a new type of digital deception the player must identify
- **Three suspects** — increased complexity over Case 1's two suspects
- **Technical evidence** — message metadata showing scheduling timestamps

## 4. CASE #003 — THE LAST ENCORE

### 4.1 Synopsis

Legendary jazz musician **Victor "Sax" Delacroix** (56) collapses and dies backstage at the historic Blue Moon Jazz Club during his farewell concert. The cause: a rare poison administered through his reed moistener — the cup of water every saxophone player keeps nearby.

### 4.2 Characters

| Character | Role | Age | Occupation | Relationship |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Victor Delacroix | Victim | 56 | Jazz Musician | — |
| Camille Laurent | Suspect | 34 | Club Manager | Protégé |
| Theo Barnes | **Culprit** | 48 | Rival Musician | Former rival |
| Nadia Delacroix | Suspect | 52 | Jazz Singer | Ex-wife |

### 4.3 Core Mystery

Theo Barnes, a once-promising musician whose career Victor destroyed through a public humiliation 15 years ago, has spent years nursing his grudge. When he learned Victor was performing a farewell concert at the Blue Moon, he saw his chance. Using his background in pharmaceutical work (his day job after his music career ended), Theo obtained a rare cardiac glycoside and slipped it into Victor's reed moistener backstage.

### 4.4 Timeline of Events

```
17:00  Camille opens the club, prepares backstage
18:00  Victor arrives, sets up backstage (reed cup placed)
18:30  Nadia arrives, argues with Victor about royalties
19:00  Theo arrives, claims he came to "make peace"
19:15  Theo goes backstage alone (security cam gap)
19:30  Concert begins, Victor performs first set
20:00  Intermission — Victor goes backstage, uses reed cup
20:15  Victor returns for second set
20:45  Victor collapses on stage during final number
21:00  Paramedics arrive, Victor pronounced dead
```

### 4.5 Evidence Map

| Evidence Type | Item | Key Clue |
|---|---|---|
| Messages | Victor ↔ Camille thread | Club sale plans |
| Messages | Victor ↔ Theo thread | Theo's "reconciliation" request |
| Messages | Victor ↔ Nadia thread | Royalty dispute |
| Photos | Backstage area | Reed cup in frame |
| Photos | Security cam (hallway) | Theo near backstage at 19:15 |
| Photos | Concert crowd photo | Nadia visible in audience during murder window |
| Calls | Theo → Victor | Arranged backstage meeting |
| Calls | Nadia → Camille | Nadia's alibi call |
| GPS | Theo's phone | At club, near backstage 19:10-19:25 |
| GPS | Camille's phone | On stage area all evening |
| Documents | Toxicology report | Rare cardiac glycoside |
| Documents | Club sale agreement | Victor selling the Blue Moon |

### 4.6 Solution Logic

1. **Poison Knowledge:** Toxicology reveals a rare cardiac glycoside — not something easily obtained. Theo's pharmaceutical background gives him unique access.
2. **Backstage Access:** Security camera and GPS place Theo backstage during the 19:15 gap — the only unmonitored window when the reed cup was accessible.
3. **The Threatening Message:** Theo's messages to Victor reveal barely concealed rage beneath a facade of reconciliation. "You'll remember this night" takes on sinister meaning.
4. **Alibis Eliminate Others:** Camille was on stage managing the show. Nadia was in the audience (visible in crowd photo and confirmed by other attendees).

**Required connections for accusation:**
- Toxicology (rare poison) → Theo's pharmacy background
- Theo backstage at 19:15 → Reed cup as delivery method
- Theo's threatening message → Long-standing grudge motive

### 4.7 Red Herrings

- Camille's motive (club sale threatens her livelihood) — but alibi is airtight (on stage)
- Nadia's motive (bitter divorce, royalties dispute) — but she was in the audience
- Victor's health issues — initially suggests natural causes

### 4.8 New Mechanics Introduced

- **Poison as murder weapon** — non-obvious cause of death requiring document analysis
- **Crowd photo analysis** — using photos to confirm alibis (not just find evidence)
- **Deeper interrogation trees** — all three suspects have more complex branching dialogues
- **Security camera gaps** — teaches players to look for what cameras DIDN'T capture

## 5. CASE DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### 5.1 The 3-Layer Evidence Rule

Every case follows the **3-Layer Rule** for the culprit:

```
Layer 1: OPPORTUNITY  — Physical presence at the scene during the crime window
Layer 2: MOTIVE       — A compelling reason to commit the crime
Layer 3: METHOD       — Access to the means of killing (weapon, poison, etc.)
```

A valid accusation requires evidence from all three layers.

### 5.2 Red Herring Design

Each non-culprit suspect should have:
- **1-2 apparent motives** that make them plausible suspects
- **1 solid alibi** that the player can verify through evidence
- **At least 1 suspicious behavior** that has an innocent explanation

This ensures the player genuinely considers each suspect before reaching the correct conclusion.

### 5.3 Evidence Discovery Flow

Cases are designed so evidence is discovered in roughly this order:

```
Phase 1: Surface Evidence     — Messages, contacts, basic photos
Phase 2: Deeper Investigation — Call transcripts, GPS data, document analysis
Phase 3: Contradictions        — Cross-referencing reveals lies and timeline gaps
Phase 4: Confrontation         — Interrogations with evidence unlock confessions
Phase 5: Accusation            — All required connections made
```

### 5.4 Emotional Pacing

```
Opening:     Curiosity   — "Who could have done this?"
Early Game:  Suspicion   — "Everyone seems suspicious..."
Mid Game:    Confusion   — "Wait, that doesn't add up..."
Late Game:   Clarity     — "It HAS to be them!"
Resolution:  Satisfaction — "I knew it all along."
```

### 5.5 Interrogation Design

Each suspect interrogation follows these rules:
- **Open questions** are always available (no evidence required)
- **Evidence questions** require specific clues to unlock
- **Pressure questions** unlock after making key connections
- **The culprit** has the deepest dialogue tree with the most branching
- **Innocent suspects** have shorter trees that resolve their alibis

## 7. CASE FILE LOCATIONS

All case data is stored as JSON in the `/cases/` directory:

```
cases/
├── case_001/
│   └── case.json          # The Silent Witness
├── case_002/
│   └── case.json          # Digital Ghosts
├── case_003/
│   └── case.json          # The Last Encore
```

Each `case.json` follows the schema defined in **Part 4 — Data-Driven Case Format**. The game engine loads these files at runtime, requiring zero code changes to add new cases.

### Adding New Cases

To add a new case:
1. Create a new directory: `cases/case_XXX/`
2. Create `case.json` following the schema in Part 4
3. Ensure all `clue_id` references are internally consistent
4. Run the validation function (see Part 4, Section 13)
5. The case will automatically appear in the case selection screen
